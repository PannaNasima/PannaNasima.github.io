Cloud Ledger Setup & Cleanup

The foundation everything else rests on. I move you off spreadsheets and onto a proper cloud ledger, build a chart of accounts that actually matches how your business earns and spends, and bring any backlogged months fully up to date.

  • A chart of accounts designed around your business, not a generic template
  • Opening balances established and verified against source documents

Who it's for

A live cloud ledger, fully reconciled to the date we agree, with every account verified against a real statement — plus a short written summary of what I found, what I corrected, and anything you should know about your historic records.

Daily & Weekly Transaction Categorization

The unglamorous work that makes every report downstream trustworthy. Income and expenses coded accurately and consistently, on a rhythm we agree, so your numbers are never more than a few days stale.

What's included

Bank & Credit Card Reconciliation

Every account matched line by line against the real statement, every month. Where the ledger and the bank disagree, I find out why rather than forcing a balancing entry — which is how errors get buried for years.

What's included

  • Full reconciliation of every bank account, monthly
  • Credit card and payment-processor accounts reconciled on the same cycle
  • Discrepancies investigated and traced to source
  • Duplicate, missing and mis-posted transactions corrected
  • Uncleared items reviewed rather than left to accumulate
  • A reconciliation report kept on file for every period

Why it matters

  • An unreconciled ledger makes every report built on it unreliable
  • It is the first thing a lender or auditor checks
  • It is how fraud, double payments and bank errors actually get caught
  • Fixing a year of drift costs far more than staying current

Financial Reporting & Analysis

Most bookkeepers stop at producing the statements. I spent eight years reading them for a living — writing credit ratings at CRISL and underwriting commercial loans at BRAC Bank. So your month-end pack arrives with an actual interpretation attached.

What's included

  • Monthly Profit & Loss statement
  • Balance Sheet as at period end
  • Cash Flow statement and trend
  • Comparison against prior period and year to date
  • Accounts receivable and payable aging summaries
  • A written commentary in plain English on what moved and why

The analyst's layer

  • Margin and expense-ratio trends tracked month over month
  • Cash flow read the way a lender reads it, before you need financing
  • Early warning on the patterns that precede a cash squeeze
  • The classification issues that would weaken a credit application, flagged early

What you get

A month-end pack delivered on a fixed schedule, with a one-page summary at the front telling you the three things worth your attention. If something in the numbers concerns me, you hear about it that month — not at year end.

Accounts Payable & Receivable

Knowing exactly who you owe and who owes you — and when. Most cash flow crises in small businesses are not profitability problems at all; they are receivables that quietly aged past ninety days while nobody was watching.

Payables

  • Vendor bills recorded accurately and on time
  • Payment scheduling so nothing is missed or paid twice
  • Vendor statements reconciled against your ledger
  • Payables aging report each month

Receivables

  • Client invoices raised and issued on schedule
  • Payments matched and applied against the right invoice
  • Aging tracked with overdue accounts surfaced early
  • Reminder cadence for outstanding balances

Payroll Support

Payroll recorded correctly in the ledger and employee records kept in order, so the person who files your returns receives clean, complete figures instead of a reconstruction project.

What's included

  • Wage, deduction and contribution accounts kept accurate
  • Employee and contractor payment records maintained

Good to know
